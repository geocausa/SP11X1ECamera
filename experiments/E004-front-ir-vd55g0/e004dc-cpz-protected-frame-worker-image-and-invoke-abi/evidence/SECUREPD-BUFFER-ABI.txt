libloadalgo_skel.so is the proxy/control half. Its loadalgo_physbuffer path:
  1. requires the proxy to be initialized;
  2. resolves a FastRPC fd through HAP_mmap_get;
  3. obtains/uses the physical-address identity;
  4. sends {paddr,len,type} over proxy_to_secure_mb;
  5. waits for secure_to_proxy_mb response;
  6. reports whether SecurePD accepted/processed the protected buffer.

Its Gaussian request carries source, destination, dimensions/stride, and heap state in the 96-byte packet.

This is direct same-machine proof of a host/proxy -> SecurePD protected-buffer message ABI using physical-buffer identity plus a typed secure-channel state machine.
