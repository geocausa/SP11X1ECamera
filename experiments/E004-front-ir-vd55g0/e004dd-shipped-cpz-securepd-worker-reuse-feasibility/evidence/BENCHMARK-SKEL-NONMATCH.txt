E004dd LIBBENCHMARK REUSE CHECK

libbenchmark_skel.so is statically trusted by the exact root qccdsp policy and exports benchmark_skel_handle_invoke.
Fresh Ghidra decompilation of the complete skel dispatcher shows callable cases for:
  dilate5x5 / dilate3x3
  integrate
  GEMM open/run/close
  gaussian7x7
  bilateral filters
  convolution/depthwise convolution
  fast9, FFT, histogram, NCC, box filter, Harris corner, Sobel
  clock/query/crash/perf operations

The binary also contains global utility symbols:
  addTwoVectorsTogether
  Vmemset

However, neither utility is referenced by benchmark_skel_handle_invoke. A search limited to the skel handler finds no call to addTwoVectorsTogether or Vmemset and no generic copy/fill method.

Therefore the trusted benchmark interface cannot be repurposed as an arbitrary source->destination frame-copy or tail-fill RPC without changing the module or invoking an unexposed internal symbol.
